Custom lists are named groups of terms kept in Philter, as your Philter user. A policy refers to one as list:name in a custom dictionary's terms, to redact them, or in its ignored terms, to leave them alone. Batches use the lists of Philter Workspace's own Philter user, which an administrator reaches with the Owner filter. As an administrator, show another user's lists, or every user's, with the Owner filter, which needs ADMIN_CROSS_USER_ACCESS_ENABLED=true in Philter.
