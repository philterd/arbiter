New policy

Managed policies are ready-made policies for common needs. They cannot be changed or deleted. Create a policy from one to redact with it. Philter keeps a deleted policy's history. Restore it as it was when it was deleted, or from any revision in its history. Batches are redacted with Philter Workspace's own Philter key, so they use the policies of that key's user, listed here. To change them, an administrator shows that user's policies with the Owner filter on the Policies list, which needs ADMIN_CROSS_USER_ACCESS_ENABLED=true in Philter. Redaction policies kept in Philter, as your Philter user. A policy says which types of sensitive information Philter detects and how each is redacted. As an administrator, show another user's policies, or every user's, with the Owner filter.
