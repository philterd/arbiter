Philter's legal holds block the deletion and purge of Philter's redaction ledger evidence until they are released: one document's ledger chain, or all of a user's. They do not cover contexts, policies, or documents. Batches are redacted with Philter Workspace's own Philter user, so their ledger evidence is that user's; an administrator holds it with the Owner filter.

These are not the batch finalization option Keep original documents, which only keeps a document's original text in Philter Workspace when it is finalized.

Philter does not report who placed a hold. As an administrator, show another user's holds, or every user's, with the Owner filter, which needs ADMIN_CROSS_USER_ACCESS_ENABLED=true in Philter.
